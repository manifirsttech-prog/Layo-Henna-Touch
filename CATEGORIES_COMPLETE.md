## 🎯 How to Organize Your Uploads

### Portfolio Strategy:

**Minimum per Category:**
- Upload at least **3-5 designs** per category
- Shows variety within each style
- Gives customers options
- Builds credibility

**Featured Designs:**
- Mark your best work as "Featured"
- Shows on homepage gallery
- First impression for visitors
- Rotate regularly

**Quality over Quantity:**
- Better to have 5 great photos than 20 average ones
- Good lighting is essential
- Clear, focused shots
- Show pattern details

**Variety:**
- Different placements (palm, back of hand, etc.)
- Various coverage levels (full, partial)
- Multiple occasions
- Different complexity levels

1. **Regular Updates**: Add new designs weekly/monthly
